PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Traditional kirana stores frequently face two interconnected inventory management challenges: overstocking products with low customer demand and understocking products with high customer demand.

These challenges often arise because inventory and replenishment decisions rely on intuition, manual sales observations, and historical experience rather than systematic demand analysis. Consequently, slow-moving products occupy valuable storage space and may remain unsold or expire, while fast-moving products run out of stock, resulting in lost sales and reduced customer satisfaction.

Small retail businesses generally lack access to affordable, data-driven demand forecasting systems that can help them make informed inventory decisions.

**KiranaStock aims to address this problem by developing a machine learning-based demand forecasting system that predicts the expected daily sales of individual products over the subsequent seven days using historical sales patterns, product information, temporal characteristics, and other relevant variables.**

The predicted demand will subsequently be compared with available inventory to identify potential overstocking and stockout risks, helping shopkeepers make more informed purchasing and replenishment decisions.

The system will initially focus on a single-store environment and evaluate machine learning approaches such as Random Forest and XGBoost against baseline forecasting methods.

The system is designed to achieve the best possible accuracy with low compute and training infra cost to achieve engineering optimization.
